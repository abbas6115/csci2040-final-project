Filmbase is a simple application that helps users explore and keep track of movies, online on the web. It brings together useful movie information like trailers and where to watch, and presents it in an easy-to-use interface right in the user's browser.

### What does it do?

With Filmbase, users can:

- Browse a vast catalog of movies (including filters)
- Watch trailers directly from the app
- See which streaming services offer a movie
- Organize and manage a personal watchlist

### Who is it for?

Filmbase strives to reach a market of people who are looking for a centralized site for managing their own film collections universally, regardless of what streaming service the film may be on.
